Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git can allow you to document updates and modifications to when working on a project. 
It is also very useful for working on a team, so you can access and merge work done by all
group members. 


Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science could be applied to the medical/healthcare field. You could use data
science to model and predict patient outcomes for a given treatment for a specific condition.


Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging preserves the history as it takes place, through creating new commits as it
is being merged. While rebasing, rewrites the history to create a more clean and linear 
sequence of events.
